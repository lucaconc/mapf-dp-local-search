Dynamic programming-based local search for MAPF

Animations of plans of the benchmark: the methods side by side on the same instance, with play/pause, a time slider and a comparison of the arrival times of the agents (click an agent to highlight it).

  • Two rings with crossing agents: 20 agents on two rings joined by a corridor, four of which cross to the other ring. From a serialized plan (Sum-of-Costs 2706, makespan 187), MAPF-LNS reaches 801 (makespan 67) and the local search 190 (makespan 16).
  • Two fully occupied rings on a directed graph: the initial plan rotates the rings one after the other, MAPF-LNS cannot change it (its sets of five agents are smaller than a ring), and the local search rotates both rings at once (Sum-of-Costs 96 → 72, makespan 6 → 4).
